# Chopper Haul '84

A small arcade helicopter game: fly a rescue chopper over a flooded farm county, winch up stranded people and their stuff, and fend off the Buzzard Bros.

**These are test builds.** It's not finished.

- **Stage 3 (newest):** everything in Stage 2, plus Salvage Bucks and an upgrade shop (7 upgrades), 12 achievements, a Cargo Album, Settings (music and sound sliders, scanlines, auto-fire, Reduce Effects, Reset Progress), screen shake and hit flashes, and a phone layout with bigger buttons.
- **Stage 2:** the full story: 4 zones and 16 fights on a County Map (bosses: Buzzard Blimp, Magnet Truck, Junk Twister, Vulture One), 13 kinds of cargo, 8 enemies, 7 pickups, typed briefings, live chiptune music, and the Endless mode "The Long Rain" (unlocks after zone 1).
- **Stage 1:** one zone (3 sorties and the Buzzard Blimp), touch and keyboard controls, and a local high-score table.

- Play: https://jweiland1978.github.io/chopper-haul/
- Stage 3 build: `/stage3/`
- Stage 2 build: `/stage2/`
- Stage 1 build: `/stage1/`

## Made with AI

This game was made with AI using Grok Build. Design and tuning by Jeff (BlueWater78). All art and sound are generated in code.

## Running locally

Serve the folder with any static web server, for example `python3 -m http.server`, then open `/stage3/`, `/stage2/` or `/stage1/`.
